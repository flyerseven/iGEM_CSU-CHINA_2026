SAFETY

Safe by design,
not by accident.

BRIDGE's core safety feature is its architecture: the payload is silent everywhere BCAN is absent. Around that, standard containment and responsible cell-engineering practice.

Safety in the Design

The Boolean SynNotch gate is itself a safety device: no BCAN, no cleavage, no BDNF. The therapeutic signal is molecularly confined to the lesion microenvironment, directly addressing the off-target toxicity wall that limits conventional CNS biologics.

Safety in the Lab

All work follows institutional biosafety rules for mammalian cell culture and standard molecular cloning: trained personnel, contained workspaces, and approved waste streams. Constructs are handled in cell lines, not whole organisms.

Responsible Development

Cell therapy in the brain demands humility. Our Human Practices program keeps clinicians, patients, and the public in the loop, and our Model estimates dosing limits before any translational step is contemplated.

A therapy that acts everywhere acts nowhere safely. BRIDGE's answer is an address, not a flood.
