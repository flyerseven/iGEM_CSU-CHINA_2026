What We Did Offline

10+ offline science events with 300+ participants, featured in major media — bringing synthetic biology and the BRIDGE concept to schools, communities, and the public square.

What We Did Online

A continuous presence on WeChat Official Account and Xiaohongshu, with 15+ posts translating the science of neural repair into content people actually read and share.

The Kindle Community

Kindle — our synthetic biology interest group — grew to 80+ members with mixed online and offline activities: a standing community that outlives any single event.

Science Writings

Durable carriers for the conversation: 2 card decks, 1 picture book, and 2 communication handbooks — synthetic biology you can hold in your hands.

Listening to the Public

5+ survey questionnaires, receiving positive feedback — the bidirectional half of the program. What we heard from patients, families, and the public fed back into how we frame, design, and communicate BRIDGE.

Human Practices is not outreach about the project. It is input to the project.
