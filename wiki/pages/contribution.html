New parts, a measurement design pattern, a modeling framework, and education materials — contributions future iGEM teams can pick up and build on.

New Parts for the Registry

BRIDGE contributes its four core plasmids to the Registry: the a-BCAN-SynNotch receptor and the matched 5×UAS-Luc/GFP/BDNF family. BCAN (Brevican) is, to our knowledge, a new SynNotch antigen target — opening CNS injury as an addressable destination for gated cell therapies. Full sequences and characterization notes are documented on the Parts page.

The Reporter–Payload Gate

A reusable design pattern for therapeutic circuits: place reporters (Luc, GFP) and the therapeutic payload (BDNF) under the same 5×UAS gate. Characterization data then predicts therapeutic behavior directly — any team building a gated payload circuit can adopt this pattern to debug with reporters before spending the medicine.

A Reusable Modeling Framework

The two-scale framework on the Model page — intracellular cascade kinetics coupled to tissue-level payload diffusion — is not specific to BCAN or BDNF. Teams working on other SynNotch systems or cell-therapy dosing questions can reuse the structure, and the software release on the Software page makes that practical.

Education Materials

Beyond the bench: 2 card decks, 1 picture book, and 2 communication handbooks for explaining synthetic biology to non-scientists, plus the playbook behind our Kindle community — an open interest group that grew past 80 members. Details live on the Education page.

Every contribution here is documented so a future team can use it directly — parts in the Registry, patterns in these pages, code in the repository, materials in print.
