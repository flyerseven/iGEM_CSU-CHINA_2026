CORE ASSAYS IN THE CHAIN

One Gate, Two Reporters

The circuit's measurement design is built in, not bolted on. 5×UAS-Luc gives a quantitative luminescent readout of gate activity; 5×UAS-GFP gives a spatial, single-cell fluorescent readout. Because the payload 5×UAS-BDNF sits under the identical gate, reporter behavior is a calibrated prediction of therapeutic output — we can measure the switch without spending the medicine.

The Core Assays

Western Blot & Immunofluorescence

Target: SynNotch receptor presence and membrane localization. Readout: band intensity and surface fluorescence — the binary check that the sensor is on the membrane where BCAN can reach it.

Luciferase Reporter Assay

Target: gate activation after BCAN stimulation. Readout: relative light units across BCAN doses and controls — the dose–response curve of the Boolean gate, captured quantitatively.

ELISA

Target: secreted BDNF. Readout: concentration in supernatant, against a standard curve — the number that the Luc/GFP signals are expected to predict.

Co-culture Viability Readouts

Target: neuroprotective function. Readout: survival and recovery of injured neurons co-cultured with circuit-activated cells — whether measured BDNF becomes measured repair.

Meaningful targets, precise capture, clear units — each assay answers one stage of the Experiments pipeline, and each result reports to the Results page.
