Simulate the bridge

A multi-scale kinetic model of the SynNotch-mediated therapeutic cascade — from molecular recognition at the lesion to BDNF diffusion across the injured microenvironment.

Why Model?

The BRIDGE cascade has more tunable knobs than a wet lab can sweep. Modeling turns the circuit into a smart assistant for SynNotch construction: it tells the bench which parameters matter, and what dose of engineered T cells the clinic would need.

Scale 1 · Pathway Simulation

From recognition to secretion. A kinetic model of the intracellular cascade — BCAN binding, S2/S3 cleavage, GAL4-VP64 release, 5×UAS transcription, BDNF output. Algorithm selection compared candidate kinetic schemes against the pathway's known biology.

Result: the model identifies the knob that maximizes therapeutic efficiency — which step in the cascade is rate-limiting, and where engineering effort buys the most BDNF per BCAN.

Scale 2 · Microenvironment Simulation

From secretion to targeted repair. A spatial model of BDNF diffusion through the injured tissue microenvironment: a 3D concentration cloud of BDNF spreading from secreting T cells, plus concentration–time curves under different diffusion and reaction parameters — including the temporal profiles seen by normal neurons.

Result: the model finds the appropriate dosage for SynNotch T cell injection — enough cells to cover the lesion, few enough to keep healthy tissue below pharmacologically active BDNF.

Scale 1 says how hard each cell works. Scale 2 says how many cells to send. Together they dose the bridge.
