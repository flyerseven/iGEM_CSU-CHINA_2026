PARTS

The bricks
of the bridge.

Four core constructs — one sensor, one gate, three readouts. Every part is documented for the registry and for the next team that wants to reroute the circuit.

a-BCAN-SynNotch

The sensor part: a synthetic Notch receptor whose extracellular domain recognizes BCAN. Ligand binding drives S2/S3 cleavage and releases the intracellular GAL4-VP64 transcription factor.

5×UAS Response Element

The gate: a 5×UAS promoter activated only by nuclear GAL4-VP64. Downstream of the gate sit three interchangeable cassettes sharing identical gating logic.

The Payload Cassettes

  • 5×UAS-Luc — luciferase reporter for quantitative gating measurements.
  • 5×UAS-GFP — fluorescent reporter for spatial/single-cell readout.
  • 5×UAS-BDNF — the therapeutic payload: brain-derived neurotrophic factor, released only where BCAN says so.
Sensor and payload are independent parts — swap either, keep the gate. That is what makes BRIDGE a platform.
